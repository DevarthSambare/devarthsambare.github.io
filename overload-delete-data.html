DELETE YOUR DATA

This page explains how players of OVERLOAD (by DevSpawn, package com.devspawn.overload) can ask for their data to be deleted.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to devarthsambare123@gmail.com
  2. Use the subject Delete my Overload data
  3. Write the nickname you use in the game and your backup code (in the game: Settings > Backup code). If you changed your nickname, list your old ones too.
  4. We will reply to confirm, and delete your data within 30 days.

What we delete

  • All your leaderboard entries: nickname, scores, powers used, game version and the device identifier stored with them.
  • Your progress backup: shards, unlocks, best score, nickname, play stats and backup code.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Analytics data (Google Firebase Analytics) is not linked to your nickname. It is deleted automatically under Google Analytics retention settings, at most 14 months.
  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics) are deleted automatically by Google after about 90 days.
  • Advertising data is held by our ad partner Unity (including ironSource). You can ask them directly using the options in the Unity player privacy policy, and reset your advertising ID in your Android settings.
  • Progress saved on your phone (shards, unlocks, settings) is removed when you uninstall the game. The server backup is removed when you ask us, as above.

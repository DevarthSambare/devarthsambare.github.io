PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: 1 October 2026

This policy explains what information the game OVERLOAD (package com.devspawn.overload), made by DevSpawn, collects, why, and who it is shared with.

1Information you give us

  • Nickname: the name you type in the game. It is shown on the in-game leaderboard. Do not use your real name if you don't want it shown publicly.

2Information collected automatically

  • Game data: your scores, the powers you used, the game version and a device identifier created by your device for this app. We use these to run the monthly leaderboard and to make sure only the latest version can post scores.
  • Progress backup: your shards, unlocks, best score, nickname and play stats are backed up to our server together with the device identifier and a 6 character backup code, so your progress comes back if you reinstall the game or move to a new phone. Sound and hand settings stay on your device only.
  • Analytics and crash data (Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics): app instance and installation IDs, device model, operating system version, app version, approximate location based on IP address (country or city level), and how you use the game (for example when a run starts and ends, your score, tutorial steps, ads watched). Crash reports include technical details about the crash. We use this to fix bugs and improve the game.
  • Advertising (Unity LevelPlay and Unity Ads): the game shows ads. Our ad partners may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device information and how you interact with ads, to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. They may use this to show personalized ads.
  • Google Play services: if you sign in to Google Play Games, Google shares your Play Games player ID and profile name with the game for achievements and leaderboards. If you make an in-app purchase, the payment is handled by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details.

3Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information. Information is shared only with these service providers, who process it for us:

  • Supabase (leaderboard and progress backup database): nickname, scores, progress backup, device identifier. supabase.com/privacy
  • Google (Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Google Play services): policies.google.com/privacy
  • Unity Technologies, including ironSource (LevelPlay and Unity Ads): Unity player privacy policy

4Your choices

  • Personalized ads: you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Google > Ads, depending on your phone).
  • Leaderboard: you can change your nickname in the game at any time.
  • Delete your data: email us with your nickname and your backup code (Settings > Backup code) and we will remove your leaderboard entries and progress backup. Uninstalling the game deletes the progress saved on your device.

5How long we keep data

Leaderboard entries are kept for the season they belong to and for past-season results. Analytics and crash data are kept according to Google's standard retention settings. Ad partners keep data according to their own policies linked above.

6Children

OVERLOAD is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

7Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). No method of storage or transfer is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect it.

8Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
